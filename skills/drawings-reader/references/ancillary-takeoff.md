# Ancillary take-off (not on the HVAC drawings)

Added 11 Sep 2026 for Hub by Premier Inn Putney. These items are required on site but are not annotated on M-560X / M-5600 / M-5602–M-5609 / M-5150. All quantities are **amber estimates**. Do not fabricate from this sheet.

Source copper metres are the already-booked B01 / B02 / B03 (or basement B11 + C01, roof C01+C02+C03). **Do not lag condensate.**

## Rates

### G — Pipe lagging (copper only)

- Material: Class O closed-cell elastomeric (Armaflex-type).
- Waste: **+10%** on booked copper metres (cuts, elbows, tees).
- Bedroom indoor pair (B01): 50% ÷6.4 mm liquid / 50% ÷9.5 mm gas. Wall **13 mm**.
- Bedroom / GF / roof riser pair (B02+B03 or roof laterals): 50% ÷15.88 mm / 50% ÷19.05–22.2 mm. Wall **19 mm**.
- Basement Hybrid reading: B11 **282 m @ 22 mm OD**, wall **19 mm**. If spec is refrigerant VRF, recode the 282 m as 50/50 ÷6.4/÷9.5. Do not buy both.
- Condensate D01 / F01: **excluded** (plastic, not copper).
- Ductwork and HWS steel: not lagged as copper.

### H — Tray and pipe containment / supports / clips

- Tray hangers: 1 set per **1.2 m** of tray + 2 end supports, then +10%.
- Tray couplers: 1 per **3.0 m** length.
- M10 drop-rod pairs: 1 pair per hanger.
- Unistrut / channel: hanger count × tray width (0.3 or 0.4 m) × 1.10.
- Small pipe clips (÷6–10): **1.0 m** centres on B01 combined metres.
- 22 mm clips (basement water): **1.0 m** centres on 282 m.
- Large pipe clips (÷15–22): **1.2 m** centres on B02+B03 (or roof laterals).
- Isolated stub clips: **4 per FCU** (2 pipes × 2 positions leaving the tray).
- Riser saddle clamps: **1.5 m** centres on vertical / passing copper metres.
